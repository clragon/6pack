# 🔥 6 P.A.C.K.

Portable Areal Combustion Kit for e6: A weapon to surpass metal gear.

## Usage

6PACK is the tooling platform you have dreamt of only in your wildest, most exquisite dreams.
It contains not only your trusted flamethrower, and your sturdy baton, it also features a wormhole device, a remote detonator, a set of stamps for all needs of bureaucracy, and a visor for targetting aid.

6PACK is always at your fingertips (or maybe in your fanny pack); Press Alt+Q, substitutable with your preferred shortcut, to spawn the command palette, which can answer any wish you have ever had, once you have merely typed it. Genie In A Bottle available separately.

6PACK is driven by exploration. Its commands appear when you visit the relevant page, contextually. It adapts to the things you are looking at, such as a post, a comment, a ticket, an appeal, a forum post, or whatever other weird kinky shit you open in your browser. However, if you require guidance, try typing "help" and "addons".

## Installation

You can grab a copy of 6PACK from the release page. Once installed, 6PACK will update itself with your preferred script manager (e.g. tampermonkey).
Addons will be downloaded automatically as you activate (and deleted once you deactivate) them, but if you wanna get the full heft right in your pocket, you can download the FAT build.
